# Xetthecum Ecocultural Mapping: A Learning Journey

This was a pilot project carried out by IMERSS, in partnership with the Indigenous and non-Indigenous
collaborators who came together through the Spirit of the Sxwo’le (SOS) Coalition. Together we co-created a
participatory mapping framework weaving Indigenous ways of knowing with Western ecological science, centred
on Hul’q’umi’num’ species names and stories of place at Xetthecum (Retreat Cove), Galiano Island. The pilot
ran from November 2020 to April 2024 and is now complete. This site shares the journey of that work: what we
did and what we learned along the way.
