# Xetthecum Ecocultural Mapping: A Learning Journey

In partnership with Indigenous and non-Indigenous collaborators, IMERSS and Whiteswan Environmental co-created
a participatory mapping framework weaving Indigenous ways of knowing with Western ecological science, centred
on Hul’q’umi’num’ species names and stories of place at Xetthecum (Retreat Cove), Galiano Island. This site
shares the journey of that pilot project, from November 2020 to April 2024: what we did, what we learned, and
where the work goes next.
