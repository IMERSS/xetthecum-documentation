## Deliverable I: Online Ecocultural Map / Data Visualization

A GIS database and online ecocultural map of Xetthecum, building upon existing terrestrial ecosystem mapping and biodiversity datasets, and complementing past participatory mapping exercises undertaken by members of the Galiano Island community. In creating this online map, we hoped at the same time to elaborate an open source ecocultural mapping framework that might be adapted, should they wish, to meet the needs of Whiteswan Environmental and other Indigenous communities.

## Deliverable II: Project Documentation 

Documentation of our efforts to plan, implement and synthesize the results of this project into the ecocultural mapping framework.

## Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance Criteria were established and assessed through the recommendations of project participants through each phase in the project’s development, implementation and reporting. Content deliverables were accepted upon submission and approval by Puneluxutth’ Knowledge-Holders, project sponsors, partners, and advisors.

Perhaps more important than the tangible deliverables we aimed to produce through this project was the process of creating them. We understood that process as an intergenerational, cross-cultural, and multidisciplinary community of practice, promoting skill-sharing and the exchange of ecocultural knowledge. We hope these resources may prove useful to anyone who finds them so, should they wish to adapt these methods in their own place and on their own terms.
